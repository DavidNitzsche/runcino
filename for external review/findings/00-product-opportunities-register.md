# Product opportunities register

This is the companion to `00-register.md` for a different kind of finding. The findings register is
for defects — something is broken, wrong, contradictory, or violates a stated rule. This register is
for the other kind of observation a review can produce: something isn't broken, it just doesn't exist
yet, or exists in a form that under-serves the runner, and a reviewer argued concretely for building or
changing it. Never mix the two — a defect that happens to suggest a nice-to-have stays in the findings
register; an idea that isn't grounded in something a report actually observed or argued does not belong
here at all.

Every opportunity gets a stable `PO-NNN` ID for life, same discipline as the findings register: never
delete a row, never reuse a number, update disposition in place if a decision is made or work starts.
Every entry must be grounded in something a real report actually observed, measured, or argued from the
codebase or doctrine — no vague aspirational language, no idea that isn't traceable to a specific piece
of evidence. If an entry can't cite a concrete source, it doesn't belong here.

Each row carries: the current limitation (what's missing or under-serving today, with evidence), the
runner value (why this matters to the actual runner, not just "would be nice"), the proposed experience
(concretely what it would look or feel like), supporting evidence/reasoning (the specific report and any
code/doctrine it cites), a rough complexity estimate, the real risk if built carelessly, a recommended
priority, and what decision (if any) is needed from David before work starts. Some entries need no
decision — "build it" is a complete answer. Most genuine product ideas do need one, since they involve a
real tradeoff or a scope call only David can make.
