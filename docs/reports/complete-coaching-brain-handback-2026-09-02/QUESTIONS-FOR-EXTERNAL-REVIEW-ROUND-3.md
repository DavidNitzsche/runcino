# Questions for external review · round 3

Ten. Same runner and plan as rounds 1-2. Every one is a decision the build will
hit; none is a restatement.

**Each carries the default I will build without an answer**, so "defaults are
fine" is a complete reply, and a partial reply is useful.

Two of them (Q12, Q13) are needed to make an already-agreed rule

## Q10 · How should THRESHOLD capacity progress across the block?

The central requirement names threshold capacity and high-intensity capacity as
things the baseline must intentionally progress. Marathon-effort progression is
now specified (round 2, Q8). Threshold is not.

His threshold is **7:10/mi**, from a half in 1:34:54 and consistent T work.

Should the baseline schedule threshold *pace* progressing across 15 weeks — and
if so, roughly what, and by what mechanism? Or should threshold sessions progress
by **duration/volume at 7:10** and leave the pace to adaptation?

**Default:** threshold session *volume* progresses (e.g. 4×1mi → 5×1mi → 2×2mi
→ 4mi continuous), pace holds at 7:10 as a forecast, and adaptation moves the
anchor on evidence — the same posture as marathon effort but with less pace
movement, since threshold is his relative strength.

## Q11 · What role for the Santa Monica 10k on 13 September?

A **B** race, no stated goal, in week 2, two weeks before the controlled Dodgers
10K. It currently gets a cutback week (24.4 mi) around it.

Race it hard as an early fitness test and calibration point, or run it controlled
like the Dodgers? Two hard 10Ks 13 days apart in the first month of a marathon
build may be one too many.

**Default:** race it as a genuine B-effort test — early, cheap, and the best
calibration available before the block commits to its paces.

## Q12 · What does "achieved its intended stimulus" mean for a quality session?

Needed for **earned-peak criterion 3**, which says the key quality sessions must
have achieved their intended stimulus and explicitly *"does not require perfect
pace compliance."* That tells me what it is not.

For a threshold session, is the stimulus achieved when: the prescribed **work
duration** was completed within some tolerance? Average work-interval pace within
a band of target (what band)? HR within the intended range for the work? Some
combination? And what fraction of intervals may miss before the session fails?

**Default:** stimulus achieved when ≥90% of prescribed work duration is completed
AND mean work-interval pace is within +3% of target OR mean work HR is inside the
intended band — pace or HR, not both, since either alone evidences the stimulus.

## Q13 · What counts as "meaningful late-session deterioration"?

Needed for **earned-peak criterion 4**. Currently prose.

For a long run or marathon-specific session, is deterioration meaningful when the
final third's pace slows by more than X% against the middle third at equal or
lower HR? When HR drifts more than Y bpm at constant pace? Something else?

**Default:** flag when the final third is >4% slower than the middle third at
equal-or-higher HR, or when HR drifts >6 bpm at pace held within 2%. Repeated
means twice within the evaluation window.

## Q14 · Quality density in peak weeks when the long run carries marathon effort

His declared pattern is two quality days (Tue/Thu) plus a Sunday long run. In the
biggest marathon-specific weeks the long run itself becomes a hard session — a
19-21 mi run with 8-10 marathon-effort miles is a third hard day.

Should those weeks drop to **one** midweek quality session, or hold two?

**Default:** drop to one midweek quality session in any week whose long run
carries ≥6 marathon-effort miles, keeping the total at two hard days.

## Q15 · How deep should the cutback weeks be?

Cutbacks currently fall to roughly 42-45 mi from surrounding weeks of 56-60 —
about a 25-30% reduction. With **consistency** now named the primary constraint,
is that the right depth, or should down weeks be shallower (say 15-20%) so the
sequence stays more repeatable?

**Default:** keep doctrine's existing depth. Shallower down weeks would raise
total load, which cuts against the consistency ruling.

## Q16 · The week after Run Malibu (9-15 November)

Ruled: it stays a planned down week but *"does not necessarily need seven days
with no quality stimulus… if a session is included, make it modest and late in
the week — for example, a short controlled threshold or marathon-effort touch."*

Which, specifically, and how big? This is four weeks before CIM, so it is also the
last window for a real marathon-specific touch before consolidation.

**Default:** one session, Friday, ~3 miles at marathon effort inside an easy run —
enough to keep the pattern alive without a recovery cost.

## Q17 · Race-week structure

Currently: Mon easy 4 · **Tue 5 mi with 5×400m @ 5K pace** · Wed easy 4 · Thu easy
3 · Fri rest · **Sat 2 mi shakeout with 4×20s strides** · Sun CIM.

Is 5×400m at 5K pace the right race-week tune-up for a 40-year-old marathoner, or
would something closer to race effort (e.g. 2-3 × 1 km at marathon pace) serve
better?

**Default:** keep the 400s. Short, fast, low-cost, and it wakes the legs up
without rehearsing fatigue.

## Q18 · The taper long runs

Currently **18 mi** 14 days out and **13 mi** 7 days out, against a 60-mile peak

Is 18 two weeks out right, or too much for this runner given consistency is the
constraint?

**Default:** keep 18/13. It matches the taper doctrine the external review

## Q19 · Do the strides do a job, and should they progress?

Nearly every easy day carries **6×20s strides**. They never change across 15
weeks.

Are they earning their place, and should they progress in count, duration or
surface (e.g. adding light hill strides in the base weeks)?

**Default:** keep them constant. They are cheap neuromuscular maintenance, and
progressing them adds a lever with no evidence contract behind it.

## Q20 · What evidence should move the THRESHOLD anchor?

The parallel of round 2's Q8, for the lever that prices most of his quality work.
His threshold moved 430 → 420 → 430 s/mi within a day earlier in this project,
off single sessions, which is what motivated the whole evidence-engine rebuild.

What should be required before the threshold anchor moves — how many sessions, in
what window, and does a tune-up race result count differently from a training
session?

**Default:** two independent qualifying sessions within 21 days, or one race
result at 10 km or longer. A single training session never moves it.
