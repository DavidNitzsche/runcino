### 12.1 · Is the lookback-extension mechanism right as designed?

**The problem it solves:** the adaptation gates ask "has this runner
recently demonstrated capability" using a fixed 28-day window. For your
account, that window landed almost entirely inside your AFC taper +
post-race recovery block (2026-08-02 → 2026-08-30) — 1 representative day
out of 28. The gate read that as "insufficient evidence," not because you'd
been inconsistent, but because the window happened to land on a stretch the
app itself told you to go easy through.

**What it actually does:** widens the search window (up to a cap) to find
more *ordinary* training days, while permanently excluding every taper/
recovery/race day it passes over, no matter how far back it goes — reusing
the same exclusion logic `normal-window.ts` already applies everywhere else
in the app. It is never "count the taper," always "skip past it and keep
looking." On your data: 28d→56d, found 5 quality sessions instead of 1, 4
controlled — that's what let PACE clear.

**The safeguard:** evidence found further back is discounted by a
confidence half-life on the evidence's *age*, not its magnitude — older
evidence lowers trust, not the underlying number. On your data: 0.976 for
pace (barely moved), 0.800 for duration (moved more).

**The bound:** capped at 120 days, itself derived from the doctrine's own
recovery/taper tables (longest realistic prescribed block, plus a base
window) rather than picked arbitrarily.

**What's genuinely unresolved, flagged by the agent that built it, not
settled by anyone:**
- Is 120 days the right outer bound, or should it be tighter (90) or looser?
- Is a half-life decay the right *shape* for staleness, or should something
  else govern how much older evidence is trusted?
- Does it feel right in principle that a runner can clear an evidence bar
  using training from up to ~2 months back, age-discounted, rather than
  requiring something fresher?

**Decision needed:** approve as built, adjust the bound/decay shape, or
reject in favor of a different mechanism for point 1 of the original review.

plan composition for every runner on the app immediately, not just in
shadow mode.
