## What this cannot tell you

Reproduced from the script's own header so the table is never read without it:

- A wrong input produces a confident, well-formed, wrong row, and nothing here
  would notice. Every criticism `build-input.ts` makes of itself applies.
- It counts where proposals land. It says nothing about whether the season that
  followed would have been better for him.
- The provisional ceiling is a sensitivity probe, not a demand model.
- Weekly boundaries only. A session-boundary proposal is deferred by the cadence
  rule under every reading, so including them would add identical rows to every
  column.
