What changed

One status vocabulary. TargetsView currently ships three: the StatusPill ("On track / Watching / Off track"), the tier word ("AHEAD / BEHIND …"), and prose ("on pace / off pace"), and Today's GAP tile derives its own. The proposal: the tier word plus the gap becomes the only status chip, everywhere: AHEAD, ON PACE, WATCHING, BEHIND, each with the gap value. It obeys the label grammar rule (one word plus optional context) and it carries the number, which the pill never did. Pill and prose retire.

The renegotiation card mounts inside THE PATH, directly under the number line that justifies it, not floating at the top of the page. Same CoachDecisionCard chrome as Decision 2.

THE WORK gets an explicit between-blocks state instead of rendering an empty week list during recovery. It names the window and the block-open date.

RACES splits into CALENDAR and RESULTS. Calendar rows carry role chips: A goal race, B tune-up with race day in your plan and an own goal where set, C converts a quality day. Results rows carry the result, its provenance chip, and the retro link. AFC shows the provisional amber chip until confirmed; the deck leaves earlier confirmed rows schematic because their times are not in this session's evidence.

Decision 3

Three calls: (a) adopt the beat order with renegotiation mounted in THE PATH, (b) pick the single status vocabulary (proposed: tier word + gap, retire pill and prose), (c) approve the CALENDAR / RESULTS split with role chips. Default if unruled: all three as mocked.

Decision 7

Palette ruling · the ladder green

Brief v2 retired #14C08C (easy → #3EBD41). But constants.ts still ships #14C08C as the zone and season categorical, byte-synced with iPhone Theme.swift, across roughly 26 call sites. The brief and the code cannot both be right. Your ruling settles it.

Option A · sanction #14C08C as the ladder categorical
An addendum names it a categorical group for zone and season ladders only, like the phase-identity palette. No code changes, iPhone stays byte-synced.
Z1 Recovery#27B4E0
Z2 Easy#14C08C · ladder only
Z3 Steady#F3AD38
Z4 Tempo#D03F3F
Z5 Intervals#FC4D64
For: the ladder green stays distinct from Good-state green, so "easy effort" never reads as "good status". Zero migration risk across 26 sites and two platforms.
Against: an eleventh hex lives on permanently and the brief needs an addendum.
Option B · migrate the ladders to #3EBD41
The code catches up to the brief as written. One green everywhere, changed in lockstep on web and iPhone per the cross-surface sync rule.
Z1 Recovery#27B4E0
Z2 Easy#3EBD41 · one green
Z3 Steady#F3AD38
Z4 Tempo#D03F3F
Z5 Intervals#FC4D64
For: the ten-color table stays literally ten. The brief needs no edit and hex-lint gets simpler.
Against: Good-state green now also means easy effort, a double meaning the semantic-purity rule exists to prevent. Migration touches 26 web sites plus Theme.swift in one locked deploy.

The tension, honestly

The brief's own text created this: it retired #14C08C and merged easy into Good-state green, but it also says one color equals one meaning. The two greens sit close enough that Option A costs little visually while keeping status and effort separable, which is why the phase-identity precedent exists. Option B is what the brief literally says. Both are defensible, which is exactly why this is your ruling and not an agent's call.

Decision 7

Rule the ladder green: A sanction #14C08C as a categorical addendum, or B migrate 26 sites plus Theme.swift to #3EBD41. Suggested default: A, on the phase-identity precedent. Either way CI gets a rule so this cannot drift again.
