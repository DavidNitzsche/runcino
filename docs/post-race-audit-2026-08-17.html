Verdict · one open loop

The question was whether this app is a good use of technology, stats, and planning to coach a runner. The honest answer: the measurement half is genuinely strong and the coaching loop is open. Every system that observes the runner works, and mostly works well. The system that should carry those observations back into the prescription does not exist. Five independent audits landed on the same shape:

Measure
VDOT, projections, readiness, adherence. Honest, doctrine-cited, correct.
Prescribe
Plan generation, periodization, taper. Well built, from research.
Adapt structure
Move, shrink, downgrade workouts. Battle-tested, seal-guarded.
Reconcile
Feed measured fitness back into paces, goals, and race targets. Missing everywhere.

The plan was authored in June for a 1:30 half (VDOT ~51.5) with bestRecentVdot: null, no measured-fitness input at all. Every tempo all summer came back at 7:00 to 7:30 per mile, the signature of a VDOT 44 to 45 runner. Eleven weeks of that signal produced zero pace re-derivation, because no pathway exists from executed evidence back into targets. The goal-gap engine computed the twelve-minute gap correctly, classified it unclosable, and did nothing, because only a widening gap triggers action. The projection engine knew. The prescription never asked.

Race day · pacing the aspiration

The watch carried per-phase targets built from the 1:30 goal: 7:08 up the Point Loma climb, 6:39 down The Drop, 6:51 through Mission Bay. Mile 1 came in at 7:09, one second off target. That is flawless execution of the wrong plan. HR crossed threshold (LTHR 162) by mile 2 and never came back.

Only two surfaces showed the honest 1:42 that morning: the Targets page and its trend chart. The watch payload, the race pacing card, the execution plan, and the fueling clock all read the goal. Targets even suppresses its own "off" status inside race week, the one week it matters at the start line.

What the block says

  • You corrected the paces; the plan never did. Every tempo landed at true threshold regardless of the target ramp. The prescription sheet was for a runner about 7 VDOT points fitter.
  • Easy-day economics. Key-session attendance was far higher than overall adherence; what got dropped, week after week, was the 6 to 10 mile easy day. The plan never noticed which kind of mile was being sacrificed.
  • Adaptation was volume plumbing, not coaching. Five adaptation events in eleven weeks: two rows deleted mid-absence, one past day retitled, and three increases to long-run goal-pace segments, adapting toward the fantasy, not away from it.
  • The taper inverted itself. Hardest sustained run of the entire block (12.4 mi at 7:21, 97% of LTHR) seven days out, brisk runs at five and six days out, then four days of nothing before the gun. Nothing flagged it.

The prediction · right answer, wrong reason

The two-second miss is not the win it looks like. The projection sat at 47.9 · 1:34:54 for 53 straight days, anchored to the February Disney Half. On August 1 that race aged out of a hard 180-day SQL window and the anchor fell overnight to the March LA Marathon: 44.1 · 1:41:55. Had the race been July 30, the standing prediction was seven minutes off.

  • The anchor-fade is dead code. A staleness fade was built for exactly this cliff and unit-tested to show 47.8 on race morning. It never runs: the SQL loader cuts candidates at 180 days before the fade (which needs 300) ever sees them. The bug it was written to prevent shipped on the predicted date. Ironically, the dead code produced the accurate number.
  • Training runs cannot move the headline VDOT, by construction. Run candidates are soft-capped at race + 1.0, then the sort penalizes runs by exactly 1.0. The permitted lead cancels to zero: while any race anchor exists, five months of training evidence has no possible effect on the number.
  • What is genuinely good: the Daniels cross-distance engine carried a five-month-old marathon into a half projection within seconds, the source-of-truth ladder held, and the course-aware split math is sound. The failures are windowing and wiring, not physiology.

The rollover · four links, four failures

The complete post-race machine exists: log a result, VDOT recalcs, the plan archives, the next plan generates against the next A race (correctly CIM). It is gated on a manual chip-time entry that lives at the bottom of the past-race detail page. No prompt, no notification, no detection, even though the watch run synced tagged as a race and the app already computes a provisional finish from it for display.

One more gap on the road to December: the generated CIM plan will schedule ordinary training straight over Santa Monica 10K (Sep 13, B), Dodgers 10K (Sep 26, C), and Run Malibu Half (Nov 8, B). The generator only considers races longer than the target distance; tune-up embedding, mini-tapers, and post-race-B easy days do not exist yet.

The road to CIM · same pattern, bigger cost

CIM (Dec 6) is on file with a 3:00:00 goal. The current marathon projection is 3:31:38. That is the AFC setup again, at a distance where a goal-anchored first half does not cost two minutes, it costs a death march from mile 18. Before the block starts, the goal and the projection have to be reconciled: either the engine proposes an evidence-based goal band and 3:00 becomes the long-range ambition, or the December block trains and paces a fiction for sixteen weeks.

For scale: 3:31 to 3:00 is roughly 7 VDOT points. Doctrine-honest improvement inside one 16-week block is on the order of 1 to 2 points. A 3:20 to 3:25 band is the kind of range the fixed engine should be proposing from the data; the exact number should come from the engine once the reconciliation loop lands, not from this report.

Opening the doors · multi-user readiness

The engine is already multi-tenant: cold-start plan generation from self-reported history works, all thirteen crons iterate users correctly, scoping is clean, and the old cross-user leak class is verifiably gone. What fails is the lifecycle around a real human. The proof is already in the database: the only two real strangers ever approved never signed in once.

Next steps · three waves

Wave 1  Before the CIM block · this week

  • Log the AFC result so tomorrow's rollover starts from truth, not a stale anchor. Needs your official chip time.
  • Build the recovery-to-race-prep transition. Without it the recovery plan dead-ends ~Aug 31 and TODAY goes dark again.
  • Post-race result flow: detector (race-tagged run + past race + no result), one-tap confirm card on TODAY pre-filled from the watch (labeled provisional per the source-of-truth rule), a race_result notification category, fix the native post-race gate, add a web post-race state, and make a spent plan render as RACE DONE / RECOVERY instead of UNPLANNED.
  • Fix the dead anchor-fade (fetch 300 days, let the fade own staleness) and add the integration test through the real loader.

Wave 2  Close the coaching loop · the CIM block runs on this

  • Measured VDOT becomes the pace anchor of record. Goal caps ambition; it stops writing paces. Gate the goal-pace blend on measured progress, not the calendar.
  • Downward re-anchoring: races and sustained slow quality evidence lower the anchor the same way PRs raise it.
  • Goal renegotiation surface: when the gap is unclosable, propose the evidence-based goal band the code already computes. The accept path already exists.
  • Race-week reconciliation: if projection exceeds goal by ~5%, the watch paces off the projection (or a renegotiated goal). The stretch goal stays visible as the stretch.
  • Embed B/C races in the generated block as tune-ups with mini-tapers and post-race easy days.
  • Post-absence re-ramp (never resume as authored after a zero week), notice which kind of mile gets dropped, schedule a field test when no race has confirmed fitness in six weeks, and heat-adjust pace evidence before scoring it.

Wave 3  Open the doors · multi-user

  • Fix the invite funnel (real sending domain, iPhone-first onboarding path, first-login instrumentation).
  • APNs credentials on prod (needs your .p8), plus an email fallback channel.
  • Structural hygiene: plan_workouts.user_uuid on insert + backfill, composite race PK, drop the 'me' default, capture web timezone, wire runner calibration, clean up test accounts.
